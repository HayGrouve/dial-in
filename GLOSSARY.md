# Онова за кафето

A grind-size journal for specialty coffee: one record per coffee, holding every brew and the setting to return to. It was called Dial In until it moved onto the онова за Акаунт. The UI is Bulgarian, in "ти"; these are the words it uses.

## Coffee and shelf

**Кафе** (coffee):
One coffee from one roaster, kept across every bag of it you buy. Its brews and its settings carry over from bag to bag.
_Avoid_: пакет (as a record), зърна, bean

**Пакет** (bag):
One physical bag of a coffee. Not a record of its own: buying another bag is a Купих го пак on the same Кафе.
_Avoid_: торба, опаковка

**Рафт** (shelf):
The coffees you currently have a bag of.
_Avoid_: активни, налични, active, in stock

**Свърши** (finish):
Marking that the bag of a coffee is used up, which moves the coffee off the Рафт. A coffee in that state is **свършено**.
_Avoid_: архивирай, приключи, празно, archive

**Купих го пак** (restock):
Putting a свършено coffee back on the Рафт because you bought another bag.
_Avoid_: върни, отвори пак, unfinish, rebuy

## Brewing

**Варене** (brew):
One attempt at making a coffee with one method and one grind setting, with its recipe and how it tasted.
_Avoid_: опит, шот (except for espresso in passing), extraction

**Метод** (method):
How a brew is made: еспресо, турбо, филтър, AeroPress, френска преса, мока, студено извличане, друго.
_Avoid_: начин, рецепта

**Смилане** (grind setting):
The number or notation on your grinder for one brew, written exactly as the grinder shows it.
_Avoid_: мелене, едрина, grind size

**Мелачка** (grinder):
The grinder you set once in Настройки; every new brew is stamped with it.
_Avoid_: мелница

**Настройка** (setting):
The one brew per coffee and method you **закачи** (pin) as the one to return to. Until you pin one, you are still **настройваш** (dialing in) and the latest brew stands in for it.
_Avoid_: рецепта, любимо, dialed in

## Your data

**Изнеси** (export):
Saving one file with every coffee, brew, photo and your grinder, which can be brought back with Внеси.
_Avoid_: експорт, бекъп, архив

**Внеси от Dial In** (import):
Bringing in a file from Dial In or from Изнеси. Anything already here is skipped, so bringing in the same file twice adds nothing.
_Avoid_: импорт, възстанови

**Изтрий данните** (remove project data):
Wipes everything Кафето holds about you. Your Акаунт stays and you stay signed in. As on the hub.
_Avoid_: изтрий профила, изтрий акаунта

**Акаунт**, **Вход**, **Изход**:
The shared онова за identity, signing in on the hub, and signing out everywhere at once. As on the hub's glossary.
_Avoid_: профил, регистрация, логин, logout
