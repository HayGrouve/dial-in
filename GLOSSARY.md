# Dial In

A grind-size journal for specialty coffee: one record per coffee, holding every brew and the setting to return to.

## Language

**Coffee**:
One coffee from one roaster, kept across every bag of it you buy. Its brews and pinned setting carry over from bag to bag.
_Avoid_: Bag (as a record), bean

**Shelf**:
The coffees you currently have a bag of.
_Avoid_: Active, current, in stock

**Finish**:
Marking that the bag of a coffee is used up, which moves the coffee off the shelf.
_Avoid_: Archive, complete, empty

**Restock**:
Putting a finished coffee back on the shelf because you bought another bag. Shown in the app as "Bought it again".
_Avoid_: Unfinish, reopen, rebuy
